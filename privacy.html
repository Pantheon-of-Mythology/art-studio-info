Pantheon Art Studio

Privacy policy

Pantheon Art Studio is a personal tool used only by its owner to organize art references. Its Google connection may appear as Pantheon TD Art Library.

Images, study notes and catalog metadata are stored locally and, when the owner requests a sync, in a private Google Drive library. Full Drive permission is requested to work with the existing library; the tool limits its operations to the configured library folders. Google authorization credentials stay on the owner’s computer, outside Git.

The tool has no advertising or analytics, does not sell Google user data, and does not use it to train AI models. Data is sent directly to Google for the requested Drive operations.

Local and Drive copies remain until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked in Google Account connections. Revoking access does not delete stored files; local and Drive copies must be removed separately.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
